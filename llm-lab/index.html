Architecture explorer
Each sentence becomes <BOS> words <EOS>. Every position predicts its next token. The model uses learned token and position embeddings, two pre-normalized causal self-attention blocks, residual connections, GELU feed-forward layers, final layer normalization, and a vocabulary projection. LayerNorm includes learned scale and bias; dropout is disabled.
Word-level tokens are lowercased; punctuation, BOS, EOS, and UNK have their own IDs. Production language models usually use subword tokenizers.
Default corpus:
The cat drinks milk. · The dog drinks water. · The cat eats fish. · The dog eats meat. · The bird eats seeds.
